Machine Spare Parts

Critical and wear parts for surface and underground fleets, sourced against your asset register and held for breakdown speed.

We quote on part numbers, not guesswork. Our technical team confirms compatibility across OEM and quality-equivalent options, flags supersessions, and tells you plainly which items are on the shelf versus on a lead time. For chronic failure points we build consignment and min-max arrangements so the part is on site before the machine stops.

  • Final drives, transmissions, pumps and hydraulic components
  • Ground-engaging tools, wear liners, screens and conveyor parts
  • Filters, seals, bearings and consumables on standing order
  • Expedited logistics for urgent breakdown requirements
  • Compatibility verification and supersession tracking
  • Consignment stock and min-max replenishment programmes

Labour & Operational Services

Skilled operators, maintenance crews and project teams — inducted, certified and supervised.

Labour gaps cost as much as parts gaps. Linkvine supplies crews that arrive ready to work: medicals current, inductions complete, PPE issued and competencies verified against the task. We staff planned shutdowns, cover peak production periods and provide long-term embedded teams with our own supervision and timekeeping.

  • Equipment operators and plant attendants
  • Mechanical and electrical maintenance crews
  • Shutdown, overhaul and project labour at scale
  • Safety-trained, medically cleared and fully inducted personnel
  • On-site supervision, rosters and attendance reporting
  • Statutory compliance records available for audit at any time

Reprocessing Services

Turn tailings, slag and low-grade stockpiles into recovered metal and reduced liability.

Historic material is often an asset filed as a cost. We sample and assay the resource, model recovery routes against current metal prices, and implement the circuit — gravity, flotation, leach or a combination — with clear reporting on grade, recovery and cost per tonne. Environmental rehabilitation runs as part of the programme, not an afterthought.

  • Sampling, assay and resource characterisation
  • Recovery route modelling and bankable feasibility input
  • Circuit optimisation for grade, throughput and reagent cost
  • Tailings, slag and low-grade stockpile programmes
  • Water management and footprint reduction
  • Transparent reporting on recovery and cost per tonne
