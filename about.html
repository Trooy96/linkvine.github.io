About Linkvine

We exist to keep mines running longer, safer and more profitably.

Linkvine combines physical supply chain excellence with modern software — because availability is won in the workshop and in the data, not in one or the other.

Our story

Started in the gap between a broken machine and an answer.

Linkvine began with a recurring scene on the Copperbelt: a loader down, a planner on the phone to four suppliers, and nobody able to confirm whether the right final drive existed in the country. Days of production lost to a sourcing problem, not an engineering one.

We set out to fix that specific failure — then found the same fragmentation everywhere else. Labour arriving without inductions. Stockpiles written off instead of reprocessed. Maintenance history living in a spreadsheet on one person's laptop, and safety records in a file cabinet nobody could audit.

So Linkvine grew deliberately into four disciplines: parts, labour, reprocessing and software. Each one exists because a client needed it and no single supplier would own the whole problem. That is still how we decide what to build next.

Mission

To remove avoidable downtime from mining operations by supplying the right parts, the right people and the right systems — fast, compliantly and accountably.

Vision

To be the region's most trusted operational partner for mines: one relationship that covers the supply chain, the workforce and the digital backbone of a modern operation.
